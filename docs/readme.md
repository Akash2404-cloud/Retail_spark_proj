# Billing Analytics and Incentive Platform


## Usecase : A big company owns retail stores across the country. It has decided to incentivise its workers , in stores , based on annual performance. Also customer spending is being tracked based on which promotional offers/ coupons / discounts should be provided. The company also wants daily and monthly reports to be generated.
