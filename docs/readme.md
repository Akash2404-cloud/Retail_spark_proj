# Billing Analytics and Incentive Platform


## Usecase : A big company owns retail stores across the country. It has decided to incentivise its workers , in stores , based on annual performance. Company is also tracking customer spending based on which promotional offers/ coupons / discounts would be provided to boost sales. The company also wants daily and monthly reports to be generated.
